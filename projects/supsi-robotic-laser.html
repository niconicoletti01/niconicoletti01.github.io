Engineering challenge

Thin metal business cards were challenging to pick up reliably because adjacent pieces could adhere and separate inconsistently. Testing the real hardware exposed issues that were not obvious in CAD or motion simulations.

The project required iterative mechanical and process changes rather than relying on simulation alone.
