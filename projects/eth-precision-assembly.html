Featured engineering study · November 2026

Precision Robotic Assembly
with Dual xArm6

Proposed a fixture-driven approach to robotic assembly when target tolerances were tighter than the intrinsic repeatability of the manipulators. Developed as an ETH Zürich technical assessment; not an ETH employment appointment.

Core design principle

The robot transports the components, while a rigid fixture establishes the final assembly references. This separates robotic positioning uncertainty from the required dimensional accuracy of the product.

Conceptual fixture principle. Dimensions, geometry and hidden features from the original assignment have been deliberately omitted.

Engineering workflow

01 / REQUIREMENTS

Tolerance analysis

Separated assembly requirements from robot specifications and identified critical datum relationships.

02 / CONCEPT

Mechanical fixture

Defined a locating approach, loading sequence and vacuum gripping strategy.

03 / VERIFY

Offline checks

Evaluated workspace, robot poses, motion segments, collision conditions and software assertions.

04 / QUALIFY

Inspection planning

Defined the measurement and physical experiments needed before any tolerance claim.

Scope: This was an independent design and simulation assessment using an ETH Zürich-provided engineering challenge. No physical prototype was built or qualified. The original assessment report, source drawing, CAD package and detailed submission have not been made public.
